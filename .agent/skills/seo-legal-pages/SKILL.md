# seo-legal-pages

Use this skill for metadata, schema, sitemap, robots, and legal pages.

## SEO Rules
- Unique title/description per page.
- One H1 per page.
- Canonical URLs.
- OG/Twitter tags.
- JSON-LD: Organization, ProfessionalService/LocalBusiness, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage.
- Generate sitemap and robots.

## Legal Pages
Create privacy policy, terms, disclaimer, cookie policy, accessibility statement.
Mention no visa approval guarantee and no government affiliation unless true.
